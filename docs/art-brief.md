# Wake Experience: art brief

One look for every scene: **photoreal isometric 3D, like a miniature diorama.** Soft morning
daylight, warm and slightly muted colors, clean modern small-business office. Think "The Sims 4
render" or a tilt-shift photo, not cartoon, not pixel art.

## Rules for every image

- 2560 × 1440 (16:9), PNG or high-quality WebP. Same camera angle family and lighting across shots.
- **No text, no logos, no brand names** anywhere (signs, screens, mugs, papers). The game puts the
  company name and every screen on top of the image itself.
- **Screens must be solid black and seen straight-on** where noted, so the game can place the
  live computer and phone UI exactly on them.
- People: small, seen from behind or far away, no detailed faces.

Clutter that says "running a business": coffee mug, sticky notes on the monitor bezel, a stack of printed reports and invoices, a notebook and pen, a calculator, keys, a second small monitor or laptop pushed to the side. ## Optional videos (5 to 8 seconds, 1920 × 1080, MP4, no audio)

| # | Name | What happens |
|---|------|--------------|
| A | `arrive.mp4` | Camera starts on `exterior`, a person walks to the door, camera moves through the door into `office` and ends on the private office. Should end on a frame close to #4 so it cuts cleanly to the desk. |
| B | `to-meeting.mp4` | From the desk, camera moves across the office to the glass meeting room and ends on #6. |

If videos are hard to get consistent, skip them: the game will move slowly across the still images
(slow pan and zoom) and that reads fine.
