# Role portraits for the character select

Five portraits, one per role. The character select shows them big on the left and small in the
roster. They are in `art/roles/`. If one is missing, the game shows its own pixel stand-in.

## Where they go

Save each as WebP (or PNG converted to WebP) at:

| Role | File | Shirt color |
|------|------|-------------|
| General Manager | `art/roles/gm.webp` | hot pink `#FF4F8B` |
| COO | `art/roles/coo.webp` | cyan `#22D3EE` |
| Owner | `art/roles/owner.webp` | yellow `#FFD23F` |
| Executive Partner | `art/roles/partner.webp` | lavender `#A98BFF` |
| Regional / Operations Manager | `art/roles/regional.webp` | green `#5BD66B` |

The shirt colors match each role's walking character in the office, so the portrait and the
little person on the map read as the same player.

## Shared style (paste at the start of every prompt)

> High-quality pixel art character portrait for a modern arcade fighting-game character select
> screen, in the style of 16-bit era select screens but with a refined, modern palette. Head and
> shoulders down to mid-chest, facing the viewer at a slight three-quarter angle, confident
> expression. Crisp pixels, clean dark outline, soft rim light in cyan and indigo from behind,
> limited palette, no anti-aliasing blur. Transparent background (or solid #05060B). Square,
> 1024 x 1024, character centered and filling about 80% of the height, same framing and same
> pixel scale for every character in the set. No text, no logos, no UI.

## The five

**1. General Manager (`gm`)**
> A General Manager of a busy small business, mid-30s, wearing a hot pink (#FF4F8B) polo or
> blouse under an open dark jacket, a lanyard with an ID badge, a pen in the pocket, sleeves
> pushed up. Energetic and practical, the person who runs the floor every day.

**2. COO (`coo`)**
> A Chief Operating Officer, early 40s, sharp and composed, wearing a cyan (#22D3EE) fitted
> shirt with a charcoal blazer, holding a slim tablet against the chest. Neat hair, a smartwatch.
> Calm, analytical, every site reports to them.

**3. Owner (`owner`)**
> The founder and owner of the business, early 50s, wearing a yellow (#FFD23F) button-up shirt
> with the sleeves rolled up, a ring of keys clipped at the belt just visible, reading glasses
> pushed up on the head. Warm but tired eyes: their name is on the lease and the loan.

**4. Executive Partner (`partner`)**
> An Executive Partner at the investment group that owns the company, late 40s, wearing a
> lavender (#A98BFF) shirt under a tailored navy suit jacket, no tie, a phone in one hand.
> Polished, a little distant, sits on the board and visits two days a week.

**5. Regional / Operations Manager (`regional`)**
> A Regional Operations Manager, around 30, wearing a green (#5BD66B) quarter-zip over a collared
> shirt, car keys in one hand and a travel coffee cup in the other, a crossbody bag strap. Always
> between sites, quick and friendly.

## Tips

- Generate all five in one session, or reuse the first image as a style reference for the rest,
  so the pixel size, lighting and framing match.
- Mix ages, genders and skin tones across the set.
- If a background comes out solid, that's fine: keep it #05060B so it melts into the screen.
